About The Independent Explorer

Some people plan their holidays six months in advance. I respect those people enormously and have never once been one of them. I'm The Independent Explorer — a Londoner with a well-worn passport and a genuine belief that the best travel writing is honest about the parts that don't make the highlight reel. I've been travelling seriously for fifteen years, across six continents and every style of trip imaginable: dormitory bunks and boutique hotels, mountain treks and Michelin-starred restaurants, solo adventures and slow boats down the Mekong. What I've learned is that there is no single right way to travel. The Independent Explorer exists because I wanted a space that reflects that honestly, without the rose-tinted filter that so much travel content defaults to. You won't find perfect itineraries here. You'll find real accounts — the altitude headache at 5,100 metres, the two nights of cloud cover before the northern lights appeared, the retreat diet that tested my resolve somewhere around day three of kitchari. The good stuff too, always. But the full picture. If something I've written helps you plan a trip, or convinces you to book one you'd been putting off, then this site is doing exactly what it's supposed to. The world is large and mostly wonderful. Go and see it. The Independent Explorer
